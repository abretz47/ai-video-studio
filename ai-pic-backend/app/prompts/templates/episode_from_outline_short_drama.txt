## Short-Drama Hard Rules: Every Episode Must Have a Payoff + Ending Cliffhanger
You must make this episode satisfy a short-drama follow-up structure (do not produce an episode that is mostly setup with no payoff):

1. **Opening Hook (first 10 seconds)**: The first scene must contain conflict, a shock beat, or an information reveal.
2. **Escalation Progression (midpoint)**: The conflict must escalate quickly with high information density; avoid long stretches of empty talk.
3. **Payoff Beat (required)**: At least 1 clearly shootable payoff event (humiliation reversal / counterattack / reveal / punishment / reversal / rescue / confession).
4. **Ending Cliffhanger (required)**: The final scene must leave suspense or escalate into a larger crisis.
5. **Low-cost shootability**: Use 3-6 scenes and prioritize reusing 1-2 primary locations.
6. **Ad-editable material**: Include at least 1 fifteen-second asset suggestion (hook line + visual summary + CTA).

## Production Single-Episode Writing Contract
This episode must output `structured_episode_contract` and must be written as a shootable production draft aligned with the production brief:

- 0-3 second ignition: The first shot or first line must immediately deliver conflict, evidence, humiliation, identity, or danger.
- Reason to stay for the first 30 seconds: The audience must understand the episode goal, resistance, and information gap.
- Midpoint progression: There must be new evidence, new resistance, a new relationship change, or a new stage goal.
- Payoff: The protagonist must win or lose a concrete external outcome.
- Final button/cliffhanger: It must grow out of the current story's critical point and must not be a random new crisis tacked onto the ending.
- Write `visual_anchor` for every scene: 9:16 close-up expression, eyes, hands, phone/evidence/prop.
- Write `action_obstacle_cost` for every scene: shootable action + external resistance + cost + character choice + information change.
- Write `dialogue_function` for every scene: one of `reveal/threat/decision/counterattack/payoff`; do not write dialogue with no function.
