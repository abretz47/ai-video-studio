Rules:
- **Incremental update**: You must update on top of previous_ledger; do not rewrite already established facts out of thin air.
- **Timeline consistency**: `timeline` must increase by `episode_number`; `time_anchor` and `location_anchor` should be as specific as possible (for example, "same day / next day / that night / one week later").
- **Knowledge gating**: Record events in which someone first learns information in `info_acquisition_events`; avoid duplicating events that already exist (you may merge/deduplicate).
- **Controlled list sizes**:
  - `facts` / `open_threads` / `resolved_threads`: max 25 each
  - `timeline`: max 30 items; each `events` / `reveals`: max 8 items
  - `info_acquisition_events`: max 60 items; max 15 items within a single-episode snapshot
  - Per character, `known_info`: max 20 items; `unknown_info`: max 10 items
- Output JSON only.
