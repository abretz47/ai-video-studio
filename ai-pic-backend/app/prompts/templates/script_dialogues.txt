[Critical Classification Rules]
1. **Dialogue (dialogues)** - What characters say, inner monologue, and reactive utterances:
   - Any spoken line by a character (with or without quotation marks)
   - Inner monologue and self-talk
   - Reactive utterances such as: "Huh?", "What?!", "What's going on..."
   - Questions and exclamations (if spoken by a character)
   - Examples:
     * "The current readings are fluctuating abnormally." ← This is dialogue (something a character says after observing)
     * "What the hell... electromagnetic interference?" ← This is dialogue (the character's reaction)

2. **Stage Directions (stage_directions)** - Pure action, setting, and camera description:
   - Environmental scene description
   - Character action description (excluding spoken lines)
   - Camera directions, lighting, and sound effects
   - Examples:
     * "Old Guai walks toward the computer" ← This is a stage direction (action description)
     * "The data stream on the screen accelerates" ← This is a stage direction (scene description)

[Common Mistakes to Avoid]
- Do not place character speech inside stage directions
- If a sentence includes a question mark, exclamation mark, or ellipsis and sounds like something a character says, it should be dialogue
