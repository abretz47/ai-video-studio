{#- ============= FORMAT-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS ============= -#}
{%- macro format_requirements(format_type="default") -%}
{% if format_type == "short_drama" -%}
## Short-Drama Format Requirements
1. **Tight pacing**: at least one plot beat or emotional turn per minute
2. **Dense payoff beats**: every episode must have a clear "payoff lands" moment
3. **Cliffhanger ending**: the ending must leave suspense that drives the audience to follow the next episode
4. **Shootability**: all scenes must be practically filmable; avoid abstract description
5. **Traffic-friendly**: key segments should be suitable for editing into 15/30/60-second assets
{% elif format_type == "film" -%}
## Film Format Requirements
1. **Complete narrative**: complete a full story arc within a limited runtime
2. **Emotional escalation**: keep the three-act structure clear, with layered emotional progression
3. **Visual impact**: emphasize the visual power of key scenes
4. **Thematic depth**: explore the characters' inner lives and thematic meaning
{% elif format_type == "tv_series" -%}
## TV Series Format Requirements
1. **Character building**: deeply portray character growth and change
2. **Multiple storylines**: weave main and side plots together to enrich narrative layers
3. **Pacing control**: balance tension and release for binge-friendly viewing
4. **Payoff of foreshadowing**: plant setups reasonably and resolve them later
{% endif -%}
{%- endmacro -%}

{#- ============= SCENE STRUCTURE ============= -#}
{%- macro scene_structure_requirements() -%}
## Scene Structure Requirements
Each scene should include:
1. **Scene title**: a concise and clear scene description
2. **Time and place**: a clear spatiotemporal setting
3. **Characters**: the list of characters appearing
4. **Action**: character behavior and visual description
5. **Dialogue**: character lines (if any)
6. **Emotion**: the emotional tone of the scene
{%- endmacro -%}

{#- ============= DIALOGUE REQUIREMENTS ============= -#}
{%- macro dialogue_requirements() -%}
## Dialogue Requirements
1. **Colloquial**: lines should match the character's identity and sound naturally spoken
2. **Concise and forceful**: ideally, each line should stay within 15 Chinese characters
3. **Informational value**: dialogue should advance the plot or reveal character
4. **Voice-friendly**: suitable for TTS speech synthesis
5. **Avoid literary prose**: avoid overly literary expression
{%- endmacro -%}
