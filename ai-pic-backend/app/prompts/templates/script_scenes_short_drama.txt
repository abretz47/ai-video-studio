## [Hard short-drama pacing rules] Must be satisfied

1. **Number of scenes**: Recommend 1-4 scenes total (preferably 2-3). Do not exceed 4 scenes unless the plot truly requires it. Each scene should contain only 1 clear event/reversal; do not cram 3 different things into one summary.
2. **Location reuse**: Reuse 1-2 main locations whenever possible. If you must change locations, state the narrative purpose of the location change in the summary.
3. **Opening hook**: Scene 1 must jump directly into conflict / a bombshell / an information reveal. The summary must begin with `Opening hook:`.
4. **Payoff beat (= gain moment)**: At least 1 scene must contain a clearly filmable payoff event (humiliation reversal / counterattack / reveal / punishment / rescue / reversal / confession, etc.). The summary must begin with `Payoff:` and explicitly state what the protagonist concretely wins or gains (evidence / resources / allies / position / money / status advantage / public opinion, etc.).
5. **Ending cliffhanger**: The final scene must leave a bigger suspense beat or crisis escalation (identity exposure / evidence reversal / villain strikes back / misunderstanding explodes, etc.). The summary must begin with `Cliffhanger:`.
6. **Shootability**: Avoid abstract wording (such as "complicated emotions" or "subtle relationship shift"). Write filmable actions / events / dialogue triggers instead.
