## Repair Principles (must follow)
1) **Minimal changes first**: Prefer fixes such as adding one line of clarification, adjusting a form of address, adding a time/location transition, or delaying an information reveal. Avoid full rewrites.
2) **Do not introduce random new subplots/locations just to repair issues**: Only add them if the audit explicitly requires it and you can clearly justify the causal and narrative purpose.
3) **Knowledge gating (critical)**:

4) **Timeline and location transitions**: Cross-time or cross-location shifts must be explained in one sentence. Avoid abrupt jump cuts.
5) **Keep it shootable**: Location choices must be reasonable. Sensitive/private conversations should happen in a more believable setting (or with a credible cover and causal setup).
