## Content to Generate
Please generate the following detailed information for this character and return it in JSON format:

1. **Detailed description** (detailed_description): an overall portrayal of the character image, limited to appearance, temperament, style, and overall visual impression; do not include background story or life experience.
2. **Background story** (background_story): the character's upbringing, important events, and life turning points; do not include appearance or clothing details.
3. **Personality traits** (personality): personality analysis only, including temperament, emotional patterns, and behavioral habits; do not include skills or experiences.
4. **Skills and strengths** (skills): list-style description of skills / hobbies / professional abilities; do not mix in personality or background story.
5. **Relationships** (relationships): relationship types with others, interaction patterns, and social habits; do not include appearance or skills.
6. **Lifestyle** (lifestyle): daily routine, values, life philosophy, and living habits; do not include background story or skill details.
7. **Signature traits** (signature_traits): memorable habits, catchphrases, and unique identifiers; do not include background story.
8. **Development potential** (development_potential): future growth direction, potential conflicts, or storylines; do not repeat the background story.
9. **Visual description** (visual_description): purely physical description (face, hairstyle, clothing, physique, accessories, colors, materials); do not include plot, relationships, or background story.
10. **Suggested tags** (suggested_tags): 5-10 keyword tags describing the character; do not write full sentences.
