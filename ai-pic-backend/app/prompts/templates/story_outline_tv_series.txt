## Generated content
Please create a complete story outline and return it in JSON format, including the following:

1. **Story premise** (`premise`): the core concept and foundational setup of the story
2. **Detailed synopsis** (`synopsis`): a complete story summary covering beginning, development, reversal, and resolution, while reflecting sustainable episodic space
3. **Main conflict** (`main_conflict`): the story's core conflict and contradiction (main line + expandable subplots)
4. **Resolution** (`resolution`): the resolution direction of the main conflict (it may be open-ended / stage-based to support multi-episode expansion)
5. **Character relationships** (`character_relationships`): the relationship web among the main characters
6. **Main character information** (`main_characters`): a reorganized version of the main character information
7. **Plot structure** (`plot_structure`): the story's three-act structure breakdown (used as the overall narrative skeleton)
8. **Core values** (`core_values`): the values and meaning the story conveys
9. **Visual style** (`visual_style`): suggested visual presentation style (camera / art direction / lighting)
10. **Selling points** (`selling_points`): story highlights and elements that attract viewers
11. **Payoff rhythm** (`hook_plan`): opening hook, emotional buildup/release, key reversal planning (if available)
12. **Twist density** (`twist_density`): target twist density (text description is fine, if available)
13. **Cliffhanger plan** (`cliffhanger_plan`): key cliffhanger / suspense list (if available)
14. **Ad assets** (`ad_snippets`): hooks, visual summaries, and CTA suggestions for 15/30/60-second assets (if available)

## Creative principles (TV/web series)
- Clear structure: the main line is explicit, subplots serve the main line, and progression can unfold episode by episode
- Sustainable characters: motivations are clear, and relationships can evolve
- Episodic pacing: each episode should support a continuous viewing feel of "opening - development - turn - hook"
- Practicality: suitable for shooting and production (scenes, characters, and conflicts can all be realized)
