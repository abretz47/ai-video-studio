## [Mandatory short-drama requirements] Every episode must contain a payoff
Please design the entire short drama as a serialized structure with "a payoff in every episode + an ending cliffhanger":

1. **Opening hook**: Within the first 10 seconds, a high-conflict / high-information event must occur (catching cheating, betrayal, identity exposure, benefit collapse, etc.).
2. **Payoff landing**: Every episode must have at least 1 clearly filmable payoff event (humiliation reversal / counterattack / reveal / punishment / reversal / confession / rescue, etc.), not just abstract emotion.
3. **Ending cliffhanger**: Every episode ending must leave a bigger suspense beat or crisis escalation that strongly drives the next episode.
4. **Clipability**: Payoff beats and cliffhangers should be splittable into 15/30/60-second ad assets (dialogue hook + visual action + CTA).

## [production story-room brief] Production contract after research mapping
You are not writing a "story intro". You are delivering an executable short-drama story-room brief for later episode writing, script generation, and ad editing. You must return `structured_story_contract`, and it must satisfy:

1. **Big expectation**: No later than Episode 2, the audience must know exactly what the protagonist needs to achieve; the goal must be concrete and should not make the audience guess.
2. **Small expectation ladder**: Break the big goal into staged missions, covering at least the first three episodes, Episodes 8-12, and Episodes 20-30 stage goals or climaxes.
3. **Information-gap design**: Explain what the audience, protagonist, and opponent each know; the information gap must serve suspense and cannot force twists unnaturally.
4. **Main line established within first three episodes**: Establish the background, protagonist identity, core conflict, and audience expectation within the first three episodes.
5. **Structural conflict**: The conflict must be built into the setting and character relationships, not sustained only by temporary misunderstandings.
6. **Shootability**: Write less psychology and more behavior; every key payoff beat must be filmable.
7. **9:16 close-up awareness**: Design visual anchors such as expressions, eye movements, hands, phones / evidence / props for close-up reactions.
8. **Compliance floor**: Flag any value, legal, or rating risks; if substantive risks exist, do not glamorize or evade them.

### Good / bad examples
- Good beat: `Lin Xue publicly plays the recording, Chen Mo lunges for the phone and fails; she pays the price of suspension, but wins a board reinvestigation.`
- Bad beat: `Emotions escalate, the plot advances, and a huge reversal happens.`

Do not write only "emotion escalates, plot advances, huge reversal." Every payoff must be written as: who did what, what resistance they faced, what price they paid, what result they got, and what information changed for the audience.

Please create a complete short-drama story outline and return it in JSON format, including the following:

4. **Resolution** (`resolution`): the conflict's resolution direction / ending (it may close in stages to support serialized continuation)
