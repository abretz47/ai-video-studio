## Generation Requirements
Please generate a detailed content plan for each episode and ensure that:

1. **Overall Coherence**: All episodes form a complete story arc.
2. **Pacing Control**: Arrange plot development according to the "{{ pacing }}" pacing requirement.
3. **Complexity Fit**: Design the plot according to the "{{ plot_complexity }}" complexity level.
4. **Character Development**: Every character has a clear growth trajectory.
5. **Conflict Escalation**: Conflicts escalate progressively and reach a climax at the right moment.
6. **Scene Breakdown**: Return a scenes array for each episode containing at least scene_number/slug_line/location/time_of_day/summary.
7. **Micro-genre and Ad Alignment**: If a micro-genre, payoff rhythm, or ad creative is provided, reflect that pacing and hook structure in every episode.
8. **Timeline Consistency**: Time progression across episodes must be coherent. For cross-time or cross-location transitions, include one sentence in the summary/scenes to explain the shift and avoid abrupt jumps.
9. **Knowledge Gating**: Characters must not reference information before they obtain it (name/identity/evidence/past event, etc.). If an episode contains a first naming or first reveal, show in that episode's scenes how the information becomes known (self-introduction/introduction by others/ID badge/phone note/witnessing/inference, etc.).

## Output Format
Return JSON with an episodes array. Each episode must include:

1. **episode_number**: Episode number
2. **title**: Episode title
3. **summary**: Episode summary (150-300 words)
4. **plot_points**: List of major plot points
5. **character_arcs**: Character development plan
6. **conflicts**: Conflicts in this episode
7. **scene_count**: Estimated number of scenes
8. **emotional_tone**: Emotional tone
9. **key_moments**: Description of key moments
10. **cliffhanger**: Cliffhanger setup (if applicable)
11. **hook_plan**: Payoff/hook rhythm plan (if applicable)
12. **twist_density**: Twist density target (if applicable)
13. **cliffhanger_plan**: Cliffhanger/hook-point plan (if applicable)
14. **ad_snippets**: Ad creative suggestions (if applicable)
15. **scenes**: Scene list (array), each containing scene_number, slug_line, location, time_of_day, summary
16. **scene_count matches scenes**: scene_count must equal the length of the scenes array; scene_number must increase continuously from 1, and all scenes may not be numbered 1

## Plot Development Guidance
- Episode 1: Introduce the characters and background, establish the world-setting
- First third: Build character relationships and lay conflict setup
- Middle third: Develop conflicts, advance the plot, and grow the characters
- Final third: Bring conflicts to a climax, resolve key problems, and elevate the emotional impact
- Final episode: Conclude the story, land the characters' destinies, and reinforce the theme
