## Hard Short-Drama Rule: Every Episode Must Have a Payoff + Ending Hook
Every episode must follow the structure below (do not create episodes that are "mostly setup" without a payoff):

1. **Opening Hook**: The first scene / first 10 seconds must contain a conflict or information explosion (cheating exposed / betrayal / public humiliation / identity reveal / sudden crisis).
2. **Escalation**: The middle section must escalate quickly (misunderstanding expands, antagonist applies pressure, evidence appears, relationships fracture).
3. **Payoff Beat (= Gain Point)**: The ending stretch must include one clear, shootable payoff event (humiliation reversal / counterattack / reveal / punishment / reversal / rescue / confession), and the protagonist must gain a concrete win (evidence / resources / ally / position / money / identity advantage / public opinion). It cannot be only an abstract emotion.
4. **Cliffhanger Hook**: The ending must leave a bigger crisis or reversal that drives the next episode (cliffhanger must not be empty).
5. **Low-Cost Shootability**: Aim for 3-6 scenes per episode and prioritize reusing 1-2 main locations.
6. **Ad-Clip Ready**: Provide at least one 15-second ad creative suggestion per episode (hook line + visual summary).

## Production Episode Writing Brief: Single-Episode Contract After Research Mapping
You are writing a shootable, editable, quality-checkable vertical short-drama Episode Plan, not a plot synopsis.

- Every episode must output `structured_episode_contract`.
- 0-3 second ignition: the first shot or first line must deliver conflict, evidence, humiliation, identity, or danger; the first 30 seconds must give the viewer a reason to stay.
- Every scene must include: shootable action, external resistance, external cost, protagonist choice, and information change.
- Dialogue must include `dialogue_function`; only `reveal/threat/decision/counterattack/payoff` are allowed. Dialogue without function is a quality risk.
- Enforce 9:16 close-ups: write visual anchors such as facial expressions, eye focus, hands, phone/evidence/props.
- The cliffhanger must come from the current plot pressure point; do not tack on a random new crisis at the end.
- The middle section cannot be idle chatter or empty sentiment; it must deliver new evidence, new resistance, changed relationships, or a new stage goal.

## Output Format
Return JSON with an episodes array. Each episode must include:

1. **episode_number**: Episode number
2. **title**: Episode title
3. **summary**: Episode summary (100-200 words, emphasizing conflict and the payoff/gain point)
4. **plot_points**: List of major plot points (at least 4 items, including opening hook / escalation / payoff / cliffhanger)
5. **character_arcs**: Character progression for this episode (at least one entry for the protagonist; brief is fine)
6. **conflicts**: Conflicts in this episode (at least 1 entry, must be an array of objects)
7. **scene_count**: Estimated number of scenes (recommended 3-6)
8. **cliffhanger**: This episode's ending hook (required)
9. **payoff**: This episode's payoff/gain point (required, one sentence, must be concrete and shootable)
10. **hook_plan**: This episode's payoff/hook rhythm plan (required)
11. **twist_density**: Twist density target for this episode (required; may use "high/medium/low + explanation")
12. **cliffhanger_plan**: List of cliffhangers/hook points for this episode (at least 2 items)
13. **ad_snippets**: Ad creative suggestions for this episode (at least 1 item, containing hook and visual_summary)
14. **scenes**: Scene list (array), each containing scene_number, slug_line, location, time_of_day, summary; the payoff scene summary must start with "Payoff: ...", and the final scene summary must start with "Cliffhanger: ..."
15. **scene_count matches scenes**: scene_count must equal the length of the scenes array; scene_number must increase continuously from 1, and all scenes may not be numbered 1
